Privacy Policy

Effective August 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the MO Gas Tax Refund Android application (the “App”) handles information. The App is designed to keep your fuel records on your device and does not require an account.

In short: We do not sell your personal information. Your profile, vehicle, fuel-purchase, vendor, and refund information is stored locally on your device.

Information you provide

The App may store information you enter, including your name and contact information, vehicle information, fuel-purchase dates and quantities, fuel type, seller details, receipt information, and information needed to prepare a refund document. This information is used only to provide the App’s recordkeeping, estimate, and document-generation features.

Receipt scanning and camera access

If you choose to scan a receipt, the App requests camera access or lets you select an image. Receipt text is processed on your device to help fill in a fuel entry. The App does not upload receipt images to our servers. If you choose to save a receipt image to your device’s gallery, that copy remains there until you remove it.

Notifications

If you grant notification permission, the App may send local reminders about the Missouri filing window. You can disable notifications at any time in Android settings.

Purchases and Google Play

Paid access is processed by Google Play. Google may collect payment and transaction information under its own privacy policy. The App receives purchase status information needed to unlock paid features, but the developer does not receive your full payment-card details.

PDFs and files you export

Refund PDFs and other files are created only when you request them. You control where exported files are saved or shared. Once a file leaves the App, it is subject to the privacy and security practices of the location or service you choose.

Information sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. The App does not send your fuel records to the Missouri Department of Revenue or automatically file a refund claim for you.

Data retention and deletion

App data remains on your device until you delete it through the App, clear the App’s storage, or uninstall the App. Exported PDFs and receipt images saved outside the App must be deleted separately from the location where you saved them.

Data security

Keeping records on your device limits unnecessary transmission, but no device or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect your device with a screen lock and keep Android updated.

Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Independent service and disclaimer

MO Gas Tax Refund is an independent recordkeeping and form-assistance tool. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Missouri Department of Revenue and does not provide tax or legal advice. You are responsible for confirming eligibility and reviewing all information before filing.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App’s features or privacy practices change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer through the WebPages repository issue tracker.
