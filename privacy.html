Privacy Policy

MO Gas Tax Refund · Effective August 21, 2026

MO Gas Tax Refund is an independent recordkeeping and form-assistance application. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Missouri Department of Revenue and does not provide tax or legal advice.

Purpose

The app helps users organize fuel-purchase records, estimate a Missouri highway motor-fuel refund, and prepare Form 4923-H.

Data stored on the device

The app stores taxpayer contact information, vehicle information including VINs, fuel-purchase details, saved seller addresses, settings, and the expiration time for paid export access in its private on-device database. The app does not request, collect, or store Social Security numbers or federal employer identification numbers.

Automatic Android cloud backup is disabled. Users can permanently delete the local database from the Profile screen.

Receipt scanning

Receipt images selected or captured by the user are processed for text recognition on the device. A receipt is copied to the device gallery only when the user enables “Save Receipts to Gallery.” The app does not upload receipt images to the developer.

Google Play subscription

Form 4923-H PDF export is offered through a $4.99 annual Google Play subscription. The subscription renews automatically unless canceled through Google Play. Google may receive purchase, account, device, and payment information under Google’s privacy policy.

The app receives purchase status, product identifiers, purchase tokens, purchase time, and entitlement information needed to activate and restore export access. The developer does not receive full payment-card details. Users can manage or cancel the subscription from the app’s Settings screen or through Google Play’s Subscription Center.

Generated documents

When a user requests an export, the app creates a PDF and saves it to the device’s Downloads collection. The user controls any later sharing or submission of that document.

Permissions

The camera permission is used only when the user chooses to photograph a receipt. Notification permission is used only for filing reminders. Google Play Billing is used only to sell and verify the annual export subscription.

Data sharing

The developer does not sell the user’s personal information. The app does not upload taxpayer profiles, vehicle records, fuel-purchase records, receipt images, or generated PDFs to the developer. Purchase-related information is processed by Google Play as described above.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until the user deletes them, clears application storage, or uninstalls the app. PDFs and receipt copies saved to shared storage remain until the user deletes them. Google Play retains transaction records according to Google’s policies.

Children

The app is intended for adults managing Missouri fuel-purchase records and is not directed to children.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated when the app’s features or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.

Contact

Privacy questions may be sent through the developer support contact listed on the Google Play store page.
